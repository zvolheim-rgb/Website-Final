03 / The person behind the camera

Meet Zac.

A reporter with a documentary filmmaker’s eye — and a belief that journalism should serve the public.

Zac Volheim is a multimedia journalist and documentary filmmaker currently working at KPAX-TV in Missoula, Montana. Their reporting focuses on local government, the courts, and the people and communities affected by decisions made within those institutions.

With a background in documentary filmmaking and multimedia production from the University of Colorado, Zac brings experience in reporting, interviewing, shooting, writing, editing, and producing stories across broadcast and digital platforms.

They are particularly interested in accountability journalism and character-driven storytelling that makes complex issues accessible, relevant, and meaningful to audiences.
